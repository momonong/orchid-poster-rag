Where do spots come from?

Local activation meets faster-spreading inhibition.

PeMYB11 promotes its own activity and PeMYBx; PeMYBx limits PeMYB11 in turn. Together, these reactions and different diffusion rates can form regular spots in the model.

Simulations propose mechanisms and predictions; experimental validation is still needed.
